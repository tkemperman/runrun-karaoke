YouTube Karaoke Settings

Turn karaoke on or off in the current YouTube tab using the shortcut or the Karaoke button next to Transcript. New tabs start with karaoke off.

Examples: Alt+K, Ctrl+Shift+Y. A shortcut already used by Firefox, your desktop, or another extension may not work.

Lyrics display

Original lyrics and translations are centered inside the video player. Move them upward to avoid existing subtitles or artwork.
